# Production Use Today

AgriFoodData is in **productive use**. The same codebase serves several
deployments at very different scales — see the case studies for full
write-ups.

## Telangana · GeoAI

A unified technical foundation for the **state government, Mandal
Agricultural Officers (MAOs), and individual farmers** of Telangana, India.
Satellite-based soil monitoring runs across all 33 districts of the state,
delivering ten soil and crop parameters per mandal.

- **Stakeholders.** Government of Telangana, PJTSAU (agricultural university),
  GIZ, BMLEH-funded ACRAT programme.
- **Data corpus.** 43,000+ cleaned soil samples, plus 600 GIZ AgriFabriX
  samples.
- **Results.** Ten parameters in production; four currently cross R² > 0.55.

→ [Case Study: GeoAI Telangana](../examples/geoai-telangana.md)

## ACRAT — local AgriTech start-ups

Through ACRAT, several Indian AgriTech start-ups are integrated as services
on the platform — BharatRohan (drone surveys), Carbonmint (carbon accounting),
Transity (farmer-facing apps).

→ [Case Study: ACRAT integrations](../examples/acrat.md)

## NaLamKI validations (Germany)

The original BMWK NaLamKI consortium validated the architecture with services
for apple yield detection, drone-based weed detection with John Deere
integration, and soil-moisture-based irrigation control.
