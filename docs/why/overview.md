# Why AgriFoodData

This section answers the strategic question — *why this platform and not a
custom build?* Use it to onboard new contributors, brief stakeholders, or
ground your own design decisions in the architecture rationale.

## Reading order

1. [The Problem](./the-problem.md) — three structural blockers that keep digital agriculture from scaling.
2. [The UN Standard](./un-standard.md) — the ITU/FAO reference architecture, adopted July 2024.
3. [The Open Standards Backbone](./open-standards-backbone.md) — which open specs the platform builds on, and what each one contributes.
4. [Production Use Today](./production-use.md) — Telangana, ACRAT, NaLamKI validations.
5. [Open Source & Vendor-Neutral](./open-source.md) — licences, governance, how to contribute.
